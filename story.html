Our Heritage

The Story of Le' Vere

We didn't set out to make ordinary pizza. We wanted to tell a story where traditional wood-fired cooking matches the bold, aromatic, and rich palette of Indian cuisine.

The Fire & Craft

Every evening, the oven is fired up with hand-selected local logs. Oak for consistent, clean heat; a touch of cherrywood for a subtle sweet smoke. By dinner, the oven floor reaches a scorching 450 degrees Celsius.

Our base is the hero of the kitchen. Made using stone-ground flour, it is prepared fresh daily. This fresh preparation gives it a light, crispy crust that pairs beautifully with our rich, spiced toppings.

"We do not compromise on the base. We prepare it fresh daily, then bake it in ninety seconds."
