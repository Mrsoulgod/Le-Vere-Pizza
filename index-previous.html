Our story

Named for Vera. Run by fire.

It started with a stubborn woman and a stubborn oven. Vera brought her mother's dough recipe from Campania in a shoebox, and refused to change a word of it. Same flour, same long rest, same rule: if the fire isn't right, we don't open.

Forty years on, her grandchildren tend the same flames. The oven gets lit at noon, the dough is shaped by hand — never rolled — and the tomatoes still arrive whole, crushed to order each morning.

We're not chasing trends. We're keeping a promise.

"Il fuoco non mente." — the fire doesn't lie The Vera family, third generation
